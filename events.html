How to register — step by step guide

  1. Create your participant profile on the Register page.
  2. Pick your primary event and, if applicable, add your team members.
  3. Pay the participation fee (waived for the early-bird window).
  4. Receive your confirmation email with your event pass QR code.
  5. Check in at the registration desk on Day 1 to collect your kit.
