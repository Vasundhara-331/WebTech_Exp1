What you can do at the event?

TechFest 2026 brings together students, mentors and sponsors for hands-on learning, quick competitions and community activities.

Learn new skills

Attend workshops on cloud tools, web development and robotics with practical examples.

Compete in teams

Form a team, build a project or a robot, and present your solution to judges.

Meet new people

Connect with other students across departments and learn from teacher mentors.

Note: Registration is easy - fill the form, choose your events, and visit the registration desk on day one.

Key information

Read these items before registering so you don't miss any deadlines or rules.

  • Entry fee: Free for registered students; some workshops may have a nominal fee.
